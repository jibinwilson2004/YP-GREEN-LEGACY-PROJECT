# IEEE Web & Brand Identity Guidelines for Organizational Units (OUs)

## 1. Logo & Master Brand Rules
- **The Master Brand**: Official IEEE logo must be prominently displayed, typically top left of navigation.
- **Clear Space**: Minimum clear space equal to the height of the "I" in IEEE.
- **Digital Sizing**: Never smaller than 100 pixels in width on screens.
- **Modifications**: Never stretch, outline, reconfigure, or add drop shadows.
- **Backgrounds**: Solid white logo on dark backgrounds, standard IEEE Blue (#00629B) or black logo on light backgrounds. Never place over busy photographs.
- **OU Branding**: "IEEE" must always appear directly adjacent to the Organizational Unit name (e.g., "IEEE Student Branch", "IEEE Sensors Council").

## 2. Official Color Palette
- **IEEE Blue (Primary)**: Hex `#00629B` (RGB: 0, 98, 155 | Pantone 3015 C). Dominant color for headers, key buttons, accents, and active states.
- **Neutrals**: Crisp white (`#FFFFFF`), light cool grays (`#F4F6F8`, `#EEF2F6`), dark slate/charcoal (`#1A202C`, `#2D3748`) for high-contrast legibility.
- **Sub-brand Palettes**: Society-specific colors applied only when designated (e.g., Computer Society Orange, Smart Village Green/Gold).

## 3. Typography
- **Headings & Primary Nav**: Montserrat (clean geometric sans-serif).
- **Body & Data Tables**: Open Sans (readable humanist sans-serif).

## 4. Editorial Voice & Content
- **Naming**: Always "IEEE" — do not spell out "Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers" or use "the Institute".
- **Voice**: Active voice; objective, professional, and accessible.
- **Pronouns**: Avoid first-person ("we", "our"); use second-person ("you") or objective institutional phrasing.
- **Compliance & Trademarks**: Use ™ or ® on first mention for trademarked programs/conferences.

## 5. Website Architecture & Compliance
- **Mandatory Footer**: Must contain explicit links to IEEE Privacy Policy, Terms & Conditions, and IEEE Nondiscrimination Policy.
- **Hosting & Infrastructure**: IEEE Entity Web Hosting (EWH) and IEEE vTools integration standard.
