## Brand & Style
The design system embodies a premium environmental intelligence platform that bridges industrial-grade tracking precision with ecological balance. It speaks directly to institutional investors, ESG compliance officers, and enterprise conservation partners. The visual mood is calculated, trustworthy, and technologically sophisticated, moving far away from stereotypical pastoral greens toward deep, architectural emeralds and surgical typography.

Drawing from modern high-precision minimalism and structured architectural grid systems, the design pairs stark white environments with dense forest undertones, sharp micro-radii, and precise hairline borders to deliver an immediate impression of rigorous, auditable planetary data.

## Colors
The palette centers on `#0F382C`, a deep, mineral-rich forest green that anchors the primary actions, navigation, and structural anchors. `#2F7C5F` functions as a vibrant emerald accent for telemetry readouts, data visualizations, and active states. Softened sage `#8FA89B` provides nuanced mid-tone contrast for badges, secondary labels, and track markers.

Surfaces remain pristine: off-white `#F9FAF9` and true white `#FFFFFF` establish an open, clinical laboratory canvas. Deep slate `#1B221E` provides high-contrast typography, avoiding harsh pure blacks in favor of an organic, charcoal-tinged density. Hairline dividers utilize `#E2E7E4` for soft, architectural perimeter definition.

## Typography
Typographic rhythm balances the sharp geometric precision of `Outfit` for large display moments with the utilitarian clarity of `Hanken Grotesk` for analytical reading. Headlines prioritize optical balance with tight letter-spacing to reinforce architectural density. Body text stays highly readable with generous line height to prevent fatigue when reviewing density maps, carbon audit records, or product specifications. Uppercase tracking is applied to `label-sm` for system metrics, status badges, and sensor timestamps.

## Layout & Spacing
A 12-column responsive layout anchors all screens. Desktop breakpoints scale across a maximum container width of `1280px` centered with `3rem` margins. The grid contracts to 6 columns on tablets and 4 columns on mobile viewports with a `1.25rem` margin.

Vertical flow follows a strict 8px incremental scale (`0.5rem`, `1rem`, `1.5rem`, `2rem`, etc.). Section breaks favor generous negative space (`spacing-3xl` to `spacing-4xl`) to allow dense telemetry data cards and technical modules to breathe, establishing a clean, commanding scientific hierarchy.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation in this design system rejects heavy, muddy drop shadows in favor of ambient emerald-tinted diffusion combined with low-contrast, microscopic borders. The surface structure is tactile and crisp:

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Uncoated `#F9FAF9` or crisp `#FFFFFF`.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Modules):** Pure `#FFFFFF` resting over `#F9FAF9`, bound by a 1px hairline border of `#E2E7E4` and a soft ambient shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(15, 56, 44, 0.04), 0 1px 2px 0 rgba(15, 56, 44, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered & Active Cards):** Micro-lift with border transition to `#8FA89B` and elevation shadow: `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(15, 56, 44, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(15, 56, 44, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Overlays, Modals, Flyouts):** Suspended surfaces using `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(15, 56, 44, 0.14)` backed by a frosted glass barrier with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`.

## Shapes
A disciplined micro-radius (`roundedness: 1`) governs the design system. Base elements, interactive buttons, inputs, and badges utilize `0.25rem` (4px) corner radii. Structural cards, analytical charts, and layout containers step up slightly to `0.5rem` (8px). 

Pill shapes and heavy border radiuses are strictly avoided, ensuring the geometry remains architectural, high-tech, and clinical rather than playful or consumer-grade.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled `#0F382C` with high-contrast `#FFFFFF` text. Micro-radius of `4px`, padding `10px 20px`. Subtle hover state shifts background to `#1B4A3B` with no change in elevation.
- **Secondary:** Transparent background with 1px border of `#0F382C` and text in `#0F382C`. Hover fills with a tint of `rgba(15, 56, 44, 0.04)`.
- **Tertiary / Subtle:** Zero-border, inline text button with an trailing micro-arrow, animated on hover by 2px horizontal translate.

### Cards & Panels
- **Telemetry & Feature Cards:** Flat `#FFFFFF` background encased in a 1px `#E2E7E4` border with `8px` corner radius. Contains a subtle top border indicator in `#2F7C5F` for active data nodes.
- **Metric Cards:** Display an upper micro-label in uppercase tracking (`label-sm`), a primary telemetry read-out in `mono-metric`, and an inline delta pill showing status.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips:** `4px` radius, `4px 10px` padding. Built using `rgba(47, 124, 95, 0.1)` background with `#2F7C5F` typography for active states, or `rgba(27, 34, 30, 0.06)` for inactive nodes. Accompanied by a 6px circular indicator dot.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Text Inputs:** `#FFFFFF` fill with a crisp 1px `#D4DDD7` border. On focus, transitions to a 1.5px `#0F382C` outline with a 2px outer glow of `rgba(15, 56, 44, 0.1)`. Typography sits at `body-md` in `#1B221E`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `4px` rounded squares for checkboxes, circles for radios. Inactive border of `#8FA89B`. Selected state fills with `#0F382C` carrying a crisp white 1.5px checkmark vector.

### Data Tables & Lists
- Row items separated by single hairline border `1px solid #EAEFEA`. Hover state shifts row surface to `#F6F9F7`. Column headers employ `label-sm` in all-caps slate `#58635D`.
